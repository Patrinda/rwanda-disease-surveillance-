    Report

    Facility staff register a patient and file a case report for a disease in a district. Every report is checked for valid dates, ages and duplicates.

    Detect

    Each week's cases are compared with the average of the four weeks before. A big jump creates a Watch or an Alert for that disease and district.

    Respond

    District officers review the alert on the map and dashboard, declare an outbreak and follow it until it is closed.
